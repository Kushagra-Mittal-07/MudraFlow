# 05. UI specification

## 0. What the reference screenshots are for (and what they are not)

`design-reference/` holds four screenshots. Use them **only as a visual-language reference**:

- the colour palette
- the three fonts and how each is used (serif headings and balance, monospace figures and labels, sans body)
- sharp corners, hairline borders, flat surfaces (no shadows, no gradients)
- the ledger-table look, the rubber-stamp status labels, the torn-edge receipt slip

**Do not copy their layouts, page structure, navigation, or content.** Where things go, how pages are organised, what the navigation looks like and which screens exist are all designed in this file, for MudraFlow's actual features, the way a senior product designer would lay out a banking app. Ignore anything in the screenshots that does not exist in MudraFlow (Deposit and Withdraw buttons, "ATM withdrawal", RBI or DICGC claims, Aadhaar, UPI, a physical passbook, a Help link).

Follow `02-flows.md` for what each feature does and `04-api.md` for data. This file defines how it looks, where it sits and how it behaves.

## 1. Visual language: "The Passbook"

The product should feel like a well-kept book of accounts, not an app template.

**What to keep from the references**
1. **Ledger tables:** separate Debit, Credit and Balance columns, hairline row rules, monospaced figures.
2. **Rubber stamps** for status: bordered, slightly rotated, monospaced uppercase (`PAID`, `PENDING`, `ACCOUNT OPENED`).
3. **Paper slips** for receipts and certificates: white sheet, torn zigzag bottom edge.
4. **Ink-and-paper palette:** deep forest green, off-white paper, near-black ink, one red for debits.
5. **Three-voice typography:** serif for headings and the big balance, monospace for figures, IDs and small labels, sans for reading text.

**Hard rules that stop it looking generic**
- **No gradients, glassmorphism, blur, glow or drop shadows.** Separation comes from 1px hairlines and background tints.
- **Corners are `0` or `2px`.** No pills, no 8px+ rounding.
- **No emoji. No icon-in-a-coloured-circle.** Icons are thin line icons (Lucide, 1.5px stroke, 18 to 20px), always next to a text label.
- **No blue or purple.** Only the tokens in section 2. **One background colour across the whole site** (`--page`). No cream or beige anywhere.
- **No component-library look** (MUI, Chakra, Ant, Bootstrap, default shadcn). Build from the tokens with plain CSS (variables plus CSS Modules or one stylesheet per component). If Tailwind is used, configure it from these tokens only and disable its default palette.
- **No decorative animation.** Allowed: 120ms colour and border transitions, drawers sliding in (200ms), the stamp fading in on receipts, skeleton rows. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Real copy only.** No lorem ipsum and no filler like "seamless" or "next-gen".
- **No illustrations or stock images.** The only picture on the site is the HTML/CSS sample statement on the landing page.
- **No three-equal-cards-with-icons layouts.** Prefer ledger rows, split views, hairline-divided columns and tables.

## 3. Information architecture and layout system

### 3.1 Routes

| Area | Routes |
|---|---|
| Public | `/` landing, `/register`, `/login`, `/staff/login` |
| Customer (`/app`) | `/app` Home, `/app/transfer`, `/app/payees`, `/app/history`, `/app/receipt/:ref`, `/app/fd`, `/app/services`, `/app/profile` |
| Staff (`/staff`) | `/staff` Overview, `/staff/approvals`, `/staff/customers`, `/staff/transactions`, `/staff/fd` |

Short forms (add payee, open another account, request a card, request a cheque book, apply for a loan) open in a **drawer** on top of the page they were launched from, not on separate routes.

### 3.3 Grid and panels

- 12-column grid, 24px gutters. Panels are grid children.
- **Panel:** white, 1px `--line` border, radius 0, padding 24px (20px on mobile). Header row: serif title (22px) on the left, optional mono tag next to it, optional action (text link or small button) on the right, then a hairline. A panel body never has a second inner border.
- **Band:** a strip with `--paper-2` background, used for table headers, table footers, ID boxes and summary strips.
- **Left accent bar:** 4px solid `--green-900` on the left edge. Used on the account summary card on Home and the approved-card panel only.

### 3.4 Public and auth layouts
- **Public pages** have a slim top bar (logo left; `Features`, `Log in`, primary small `Open account` right), no sidebar.
- **Auth pages (register, login, staff login)** use a **split screen**: a left panel (40% width, `--green-950`, white text) with the logo, a serif statement and three sample ledger lines in mono, and the form on the right on `--page`, vertically centred, max width 480px. Under 900px the left panel is hidden and a small logo appears above the form.

## 4. Components

### 4.1 Buttons
| Variant | Style |
|---|---|
| Primary | `--green-900` background, white text, height 44px, padding 0 20px, radius 0. Hover `--green-950`. Optional arrow icon after the label |
| Secondary | White background, 1px `--line-strong` border, `--ink` text. Hover border `--green-900` |
| Danger | White background, 1px `--debit` border and text. Hover `--debit-bg` |
| Danger solid | `--debit` background, white text (only in confirmations for reject and freeze) |
| Text link | `--green-700`, underline on hover, with a small arrow where it navigates |
| Chip (small) | Height 24px, padding 0 8px, `--paper-2` background, 1px `--line` border, radius 2px, mono 11px uppercase, optional 12px icon. Used for SHOW, HIDE, VIEW, COPY |
| Disabled | 40% opacity, `cursor: not-allowed` |
| Loading | Label becomes "Processing…", disabled. Never a spinner alone |

Focus: 2px `--green-900` outline, 2px offset (`--gold` on dark surfaces). Primary buttons never sit side by side; one primary action per view.

### 4.2 Form fields
- **Label:** mono 11px uppercase `--ink-2`, above the field.
- **Input:** height 44px, 1px `--line-strong`, white, radius 0, padding 0 14px, Inter 16px. Mono for ID, account number, IFSC, PAN, OTP and amount fields.
- **Focus:** border `--green-900` plus outline. **Error:** border `--debit` and a message below in Inter 13px `--debit` starting with the word "Error:". **Helper:** Inter 13px `--ink-3`.
- **Segmented control** (transfer mode, account type, tenure, maturity instruction, filter chips): a row of joined bordered buttons. Selected: `--green-900` fill with white text (or 2px green border and `--green-100` fill when the option has a caption). Disabled options are 40% opacity with the reason as a caption.
- **Radio tiles** for choices that need a caption (tenure with rate, mode with limit): bordered tiles, selected = 2px `--green-900` border + `--green-100` background + filled dot top-left.
- **Password field:** `Show` / `Hide` chip at the right end.
- **Amount field:** a serif `₹` prefix, mono input, **live amount in words** below in Inter 13px `--ink-3`.
- **OTP field:** one mono input, letter-spacing 0.4em, 6 digits, helper "Demo mode: the OTP is 123456."
- **Inline password confirmation:** a final field at the bottom of drawer forms labelled "Confirm with your password".

### 4.3 Stamp and status tag
Large stamp (receipts, certificates, success states):
```css
.stamp {
  display: inline-block;
  font: 600 12px/1 var(--font-mono);
  letter-spacing: 0.14em;
  text-transform: uppercase;
  padding: 8px 12px;
  border: 1.5px solid currentColor;
  transform: rotate(-2.5deg);
  color: var(--green-900);
}
```
Small flat tag (tables, lists): same type at 11px, padding 3px 8px, no rotation, tinted fill.

| Status | Colours | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| PENDING, REQUESTED | `--amber` text, `--amber-bg` fill, `--amber-line` border, leading dot | loans, service requests |
| APPROVED, ACTIVE, SUCCESSFUL, PAID, CREDITED, LOCKED | `--green-900` text, `--green-100` fill | approved items, active accounts and FDs, receipts |
| REJECTED, FROZEN | `--debit` text, `--debit-bg` fill | rejected items, frozen accounts |
| MATURED | `--ink-2` text, `--paper-2` fill | finished FDs |

An approved debit card shows **ACTIVE**; an approved cheque book or loan shows **APPROVED**.

### 4.4 Ledger table (signature component)
- Header: `--paper-2` background, mono 11px uppercase, 44px tall, sticky inside scroll containers.
- Rows: 44px (36px in staff tables), 1px `--line` rules, white, no zebra. Row hover `--page`.
- Columns: **DATE** (mono 13, `--ink-3`), **DESCRIPTION** (Inter 14), **DEBIT** (right, mono, `--debit`, `− ₹1,10,000.00 ↓`), **CREDIT** (right, mono, `--credit`, `+ ₹4,80,000.00 ↑`), **BALANCE** (right, mono, `--ink`). Empty debit/credit cells show `–`.
- The sign and arrow carry meaning in addition to colour.
- Footer band with a right-aligned link or pagination.
- Rows that open something are links with a visible focus state.
- **Under 640px** each row becomes a stacked card: date and amount on line one, description on line two, balance on line three.

### 4.5 Masked value
- **Account number:** `•••• •••• 0001` in mono, followed by chips `VIEW` and `COPY`. `VIEW` fetches the full number, shows it, and re-masks after 10 seconds. `COPY` copies the full number and the chip reads "Copied" for 1.5 seconds.
- **Balance:** hidden by default as `₹ ••,••,•••` plus `.••`. A `SHOW` / `HIDE` chip (with `aria-pressed`) next to the label toggles it. State is in memory and resets on reload and on switching account. When revealed, the amount in words appears below in Inter 14px `--ink-3`.
- Everywhere a balance appears in a list (account list, FD form), it is masked the same way with a per-row chip.

### 4.6 Slip (receipt and certificate paper)
A white sheet, max width 560px, 1px `--line` border, padding 32px, label/value rows separated by hairlines (label Inter 15px `--ink-2` left, value mono or Inter 600 right). **Zigzag bottom edge:** a row of triangles about 14px wide and 8px tall in the page colour along the bottom, so it looks torn off a pad (CSS gradients or an inline SVG pattern; match the feel of `receipt-success.png`). A stamp sits top-right of the heading.

### 4.7 Drawer
Slides in from the right, 480px wide, full height, white, 1px left border `--line-strong`, on a `rgba(18,21,18,0.4)` flat backdrop. Header: serif title and a close button. Body scrolls. A sticky footer holds `Cancel` (secondary) and the primary action. Escape and backdrop click close it unless a request is in flight. Focus is trapped and returned to the trigger. Under 768px it is full screen. Server errors show as an inline banner at the top of the body.

### 4.8 Modal
Centred, 480px wide, white, 1px `--line-strong`, flat backdrop. Used for: confirm transfer (password and OTP), confirm fixed deposit (password), and every staff confirmation (approve, reject, freeze, unfreeze, process maturities). Header serif title, body a one-sentence summary of the effect, footer `Cancel` and the action button.

### 4.9 Banner, toast, empty, loading, error
- **Banner:** white, 1px `--line` border, 3px left border in `--green-900` (success), `--amber-line` (warning) or `--debit` (error), Inter 14px. No icon circles.
- **Toast:** only for "Copied", mono 12px, bottom-left, 2 seconds.
- **Empty:** a dashed 1px `--line-strong` box with a serif line, one muted sentence and a relevant button.
- **Loading:** `--paper-2` bars where text will be; tables show 5 skeleton rows; a 1.2s opacity pulse (off under reduced motion).
- **Error:** banner with the API message and a `Try again` secondary button.

### 4.10 Account switcher (customer top bar)
A button showing `● Savings ••••0001 ▾`. The menu lists every account (type, masked number, FROZEN tag if frozen; the active one has a filled dot and a left green bar), a hairline, then `+ Open another account` which opens the drawer. Keyboard operable (arrow keys, Enter, Escape). A frozen active account also shows a banner at the top of the content: "This account is frozen. Transfers are disabled. Contact your branch."

### 4.11 Bottom tab bar (customer, under 768px)
Fixed to the bottom, white, 1px top hairline, 5 tabs with a line icon and a mono 10px label: `Home`, `Transfer`, `History`, `Services`, `More`. The active tab uses `--green-900` and a 2px top bar. `More` opens a sheet listing Payees, Fixed deposits, Profile and Log out. Add bottom padding to content so nothing hides behind it.

### 4.12 Stat
A small block: mono 11px label, serif 28px value (mono if it is an ID or date), optional muted caption. Stats in a row are separated by hairlines, not boxed.

### 4.13 Money component
`<Money value type="debit|credit|neutral" size="table|big" />`. Indian format, two decimals, always the `₹` sign. Big variant: rupee part Playfair 52px, paise `.00` mono 26px `--ink-3`.

### 4.14 Split view (staff customers, and any master-detail)
Two panels side by side: a list (about 38%) and a detail (about 62%). The selected list row has a 3px green left bar and `--green-100` background. Under 900px the list is shown first and selecting a row opens the detail as a full screen with a Back link.

## 10. Responsive and print

| Width | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| 1280px and up | Sidebar plus content, full layouts |
| 1024 to 1279px | Same, filter toolbars wrap to two rows |
| 768 to 1023px | Sidebar becomes an off-canvas drawer behind a menu button. Two-column pages stack. Staff tables scroll horizontally |
| Under 768px | Customer bottom tab bar appears. Gutters 16px, panel padding 20px, balance 40px, ledger tables become stacked cards, drawers go full screen, radio tile grids go to one column, the Transfer slip moves below the form |

Touch targets are at least 44px. Inputs are at least 16px.

**Print styles** (receipts and statements): hide sidebar, top bar, bottom bar, buttons and filters; white background; black ink; the slip or table full width; keep the stamp; `@page` margin 16mm. A statement prints the ledger table for the current filters with a heading containing the account type, masked number and date range.

## 11. Accessibility

- All text meets WCAG AA (the tokens were chosen for this). Colour is never the only signal: debits and credits also carry `−`/`+` and `↓`/`↑`; statuses are written words.
- Every input has a real `<label>`. Errors use `aria-describedby`; server errors use `role="alert"`.
- The balance toggle is a button with `aria-pressed`. The account switcher, segmented controls and tabs have correct roles and arrow-key support. Drawers and modals trap focus, close on Escape and return focus to their trigger.
- Tables are real `<table>` elements with `<th scope>`. Stacked mobile rows keep labels via visually hidden text.
- Visible focus on every interactive element.
- Sidebar and bottom bar mark the current page with `aria-current="page"`.
- `lang="en-IN"` on the page. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
